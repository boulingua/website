Boulingua brings together language-teaching sites, a resource directory and
reading tools. English, French and German have substantial authored collections;
further language courses are in development. The available material is being
reviewed for content, assessment quality, recordings and curriculum coverage.
Published pages and a proposed curriculum mapping do not by themselves establish
a complete, independently approved curriculum or examination preparation course.

The map links the three established teaching collections and shows the wider
language family. Hover a country or capital for the map's language estimates.

## EFL — English as a Foreign Language

An authored English collection spanning Klassen 5 through 13, with 180 teaching
units covering listening, reading, writing, speaking, grammar and vocabulary.
It is organised into G, M and E tracks. Task-level curriculum mappings and
teacher review remain in progress; this is not a claim of approved coverage.

For teachers and learners at Gesamtschule.

Visit: [boulingua.github.io/efl/](https://boulingua.github.io/efl/)

## FLE — Français Langue Étrangère

An authored French collection spanning Klassen 6 through 13, with 156 teaching
units on compréhension orale et écrite, production orale et écrite, grammaire
and vocabulaire. It is organised into G, M and E tracks, with curriculum
mapping and educational review still in progress.

For teachers and learners at Gesamtschule.

Visit: [boulingua.github.io/fle/](https://boulingua.github.io/fle/)

## DaF — Deutsch als Fremdsprache

A German collection for international learners, with 60 teaching units
organised under the intended CEFR levels A1 through C1. Tasks address Lesen,
Hören, Schreiben and Sprechen. This structure describes the intended learning
scope; it does not certify complete CEFR coverage or exam readiness.

For learners and teachers selecting material for German-language study.

Visit: [boulingua.github.io/daf/](https://boulingua.github.io/daf/)

## Ressourcen-Hub — Curated Teaching Resources

A curated directory of teaching resources for English, French and German,
including institutional sources such as the British Council and Deutsche Welle.
Access and reuse conditions vary by resource; check the recorded licence and
the provider's current terms before adapting or redistributing material.

Resources are tagged by language, level, skill, and licence type so teachers
can quickly find what they need.

For teachers of English, French, and German across all levels.

Visit: [boulingua.github.io/ressources/](https://boulingua.github.io/ressources/)

## Reading tools

[Scriptlibrary](https://boulingua.github.io/scriptlibrary/) provides 18 selected
French, German and English classics, original texts and optional single-letter
practice online and in downloadable editions.
[Scriptbridge](https://github.com/boulingua/scriptbridge) applies the same
reversible practice rules to supported web pages as a sideloaded Chrome
extension. The substitutions are simplified reading exercises; they do not
translate text or establish pronunciation equivalence or learning effectiveness.

## In Vorbereitung

These courses have repositories and development plans. Their review drafts,
where available, are not approved teaching releases. Read each course's status
page for what is currently available. The list uses the shared language registry
also used by the map and [design page](/design/).
